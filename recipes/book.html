Method

Cheeseburger Bombs

Serves 6 · 25 min · Freezer Friendly

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 oz) package crescent rolls
  • 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1/4 cup chopped onions
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/2 cup cheddar cheese, plus more for the bombs
  • 1 egg, for egg wash (optional)
  • Sesame seeds, to top (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat. Oven to 350 °F; line a 9x13 baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Filling. Brown the beef over medium heat, then add the onion and seasonings and cook until no longer pink and the onion is soft. Drain.
  3. Cheese. Stir in the ketchup, turn off the heat and stir in the cheese. Cool slightly.
  4. Dough. Unroll the crescent dough, pinch the seams into one rectangle, roll lightly and cut into 6 squares.
  5. Fill. Add about 2 tbsp filling and a little extra cheddar to each, fold and pinch to seal, and place seam side down.
  6. Bake. Brush with egg, add sesame seeds if you like, and bake 15 minutes until golden.

Chili Crunch Chicken Tenders

Deep Fried

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 lbs (0.9 kg) chicken breast, sliced into strips (or tenderloins)
  • 2 large eggs, whisked
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour + 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp onion powder, 2 tsp salt, 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp 5-spice powder (optional)
  • Neutral oil, for frying

Chili crunch mayo

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup Momofuku chili crunch

Garnish

  • Green onions, thinly sliced
  • Drizzle of chili crunch
  • 1 tsp flaky salt

Method

  1. Season. Cut the chicken into strips about 1 inch thick and 3–4 inches long; season on all sides.
  2. Set up. One bowl with flour, cornstarch and baking powder; another with the whisked eggs.
  3. Heat oil. Heat 2–3 inches of oil to 350 °F / 175 °C.
  4. Coat. Drizzle a spoonful of egg into the flour and toss to make crispy bits. Dip each tender in egg, let it drip, then press into the flour and shake off the excess.

  6. Mayo. Mix mayonnaise and chili crunch until smooth.
  7. Serve. Pile on a plate with fries or rice, drizzle with chili crunch, scatter green onions and serve with the mayo.

Ingredients

Grilled potatoes

  • 4 large russet potatoes

  • Heavy-duty aluminium foil

Loaded filling

  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup pepper jack cheese, shredded
  • 1/4 cup fresh chives, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Filet mignon steak bites

  • 1 lb filet mignon medallions, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Optional: 1 tsp steak seasoning (SPG or Montreal)

Garlic herb butter

  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter

  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp fresh thyme leaves

Ingredients

Hot honey chicken

  • 250 g chicken breast, diced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic granules
  • 1/2 tsp onion granules
  • Salt, black pepper
  • 20 g honey
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • Pinch of mild chilli flakes

Pasta

  • 150 g dried rigatoni
  • 1 small white onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp chopped garlic
  • 2½ tbsp tomato purée
  • 80 ml single cream
  • 100 ml pasta water
  • 20 g Parmesan, finely grated

Method

Tomato sauce

  • 10 g fresh basil
  • Fine sea salt
  • Extra virgin olive oil

Method

  1. Dough. Mix semolina and salt, add the water slowly with a fork until crumbly, then squeeze into a rough dough. Knead 2–3 minutes, cover and rest 10 minutes.

  5. Keep hot. Spread the mash into a casserole dish and keep it hot in the oven; the top may turn golden brown.
